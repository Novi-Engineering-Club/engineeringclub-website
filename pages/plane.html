Aircraft 1: Eclipson Model T

About

In 2025, we are building an RC airplane that is entirely 3D printed. We are using cutting-edge manufacturing technologies to quickly build the parts and assemble them. This project demonstrates our capability in advanced manufacturing and aerospace design.

Aircraft 1: Technical Specifications

Model: Eclipson Model T

Wingspan: 1.2 meters

Motor: SunnySky 1250 Kv Brushless Outrunner

Flight Controller: Hobbypower 30A

Transmitter: Radiolink T8FB

Receiver: R8EF

Power: 3S LiPo Battery

Construction: 100% 3D Printed

Aircraft 1: Current Progress

  • Fully assembled airframe completed
  • Ready to begin wiring process
  • Expected completion: Early December 2025
