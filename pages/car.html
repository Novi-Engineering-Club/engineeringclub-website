RC Car Project

Tamiya TT02 BR Racing Buggy

Project Overview

Model: Tamiya TT02 BR Racing Buggy

Year Started: 2024

Status: Completed & Racing

About

In 2024, we built a competition level RC brushless RC buggy. It is built on the TT02 platform with a 4WD drivetrain. This project showcases our team's expertise in mechanical engineering, electronics, and competitive racing.

Technical Specifications

Platform: Tamiya TT02

Drivetrain: 4WD

Motor: HobbyWing Quicrun Brushless Motor (4000Kv, Sensorless)

ESC: HobbyWing Power System

Steering Servo: High-torque 15Kg servo

Radio System: Spektrum

Power: 3S LiPo Battery

Class: Modified Racing Class

Achievements

  • Successfully assembled a competition-level RC buggy
  • Competed for the first time as a team
  • Integrated advanced brushless power systems and servo controls
